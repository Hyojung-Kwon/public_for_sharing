Designing Tomorrow's Mobility on Campus

Key Highlights

1
Hyundai Motor Group and Seoul National University conducted Korea's first on-campus DRT pilot.
2
Eco-friendly and inclusive mobility operations improved student commuting and access to learning.
3
The pilot will serve as foundational data for DRT adoption at national and public universities.
Seoul National University students boarding the DRT pilot vehicle

Hyundai Shucle and Seoul National University conducted Korea's first on-campus DRT shuttle pilot as part of NUMA (Next Urban Mobility Alliance), a public-private-academic mobility alliance. The initiative aimed to address transportation challenges within the vast, hilly campus. Seoul National University led the overall research, while Hyundai provided vehicles and platform support. During the pilot, various operational experiments — including shared rides, operating hours, and vehicle types — were conducted alongside participants to refine a campus-optimized DRT model.

Students who used the DRT were highly satisfied — enjoying guaranteed seating, direct routes to their destinations, and better access to learning. The SNU research team believes this pilot will serve as an important benchmark for other universities with similar geographical challenges, and plans to continue advancing research toward a future campus mobility ecosystem built on accessibility and inclusivity.
