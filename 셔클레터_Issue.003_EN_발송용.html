Travel Reimagined with DRT

Key Highlights

1
Hyundai Shucle supported mobility for a 2-day Runtrip event using tourism-oriented DRT.
2
The event demonstrated the potential of tourism-oriented DRT in connecting dispersed attractions.
3
Shucle plans to expand as an integrated mobility platform covering both everyday travel and tourism.
A tourism DRT running through the streets of Gongju

현대자동차 셔클이 충청 지역의 관광형 DRT인 ‘C-Tour 버스’와 함께 공주·부여 일대에서 1박 2일 런트립 행사를 성공적으로 마쳤습니다. 이번 행사는 러닝과 관광을 결합하고 거점 간 이동을 DRT가 지원하는 방식으로 이루어졌는데요. 참가자들은 앱을 통해 실시간 도착 예정 시간을 확인하고, 긴 대기 시간 없이 원하는 관광지로 즉시 이동하며 자차 운전 부담 없는 여행에 온전히 몰두할 수 있었습니다.

What makes this event particularly meaningful is that it went beyond the conventional role of DRT — demonstrating the potential of tourism-oriented DRT to organically connect dispersed travel destinations. Activating tourism DRT can extend visitors' stays in local areas and stimulate consumption in downtown districts, traditional markets, and local alleyways. Looking ahead, Shucle plans to add advanced features including multilingual support, intercity bus integration, and international card payment — positioning itself as a one-of-a-kind integrated mobility platform spanning both everyday and tourism travel.
